# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release activities, schedules, and communications across teams. They ensure successful, timely releases by managing the release pipeline, coordinating with stakeholders, and maintaining release documentation.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules and manage release windows
- Manage release pipeline and deployment processes
- Ensure pre-release requirements are met (CI/CD, testing, documentation)
- Communicate release status and changes to stakeholders
- Maintain release notes and version documentation
- Coordinate with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Technical teams
- Execute rollback procedures when necessary

### Goals
- Deliver reliable releases with minimal disruption
- Reduce release cycle time and increase deployment frequency
- Maintain clear communication across all stakeholders
- Ensure comprehensive release documentation

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Product and Project Managers
- Pre-release coordination with Developers and QA
- Release announcements to stakeholders and support teams
- Post-release retrospectives and incident debriefs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate on timelines, dependencies, and risk management
- **Product Managers:** Align on feature priorities and release scope
- **Developers:** Validate deployment readiness and coordinate hotfixes
- **QA:** Confirm testing completion and deployment verification
- **Technical Architect:** Ensure architectural requirements are met for releases
- **Risk & Compliance Lead:** Address compliance and security requirements before release

---

## Risk & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Leads identify and mitigate project risks while ensuring compliance with organizational standards, regulatory requirements, and security policies. They maintain risk registers and facilitate risk communication across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Identify, assess, and track project risks throughout the lifecycle
- Maintain and update the risk register
- Oversee compliance requirements and ensure standards are met
- Define risk mitigation strategies and escalation paths
- Conduct risk assessments during planning and execution
- Monitor security scan results and coordinate remediation
- Facilitate risk review meetings and communicate to stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize project risk exposure and impact
- Ensure compliance with all applicable standards and regulations
- Maintain transparency in risk status and mitigation progress
- Enable proactive risk management across teams

### Typical Communication
- Weekly risk review sessions with Project Managers
- Escalation communications to sponsors and leadership
- Compliance reports and audit documentation
- Risk assessment summaries during planning sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Collaborate on risk identification and mitigation planning
- **Developers:** Work with teams to address technical and security risks
- **Release Manager:** Validate compliance requirements before releases
- **Technical Architect:** Assess architectural risks and design trade-offs
- **External Stakeholders:** Communicate risk status and compliance posture
- **Change Manager:** Evaluate risks associated with proposed changes

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system architecture and ensure technical solutions align with business requirements. They provide technical leadership, establish architectural standards, and guide teams through complex technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Design and document system architecture and technical solutions
- Define technical standards, patterns, and best practices
- Guide technical decision-making and resolve design conflicts
- Review technical implementations for alignment with architecture
- Assess technical feasibility during planning
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and technical debt
- Mentor developers on architectural principles and patterns

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure technical solutions
- Ensure consistency in technical approaches across projects
- Balance business requirements with technical excellence
- Reduce technical debt and architectural complexity

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews and technical documentation
- Technical planning sessions with Product and Project teams
- Code reviews and pair programming for complex features
- Technical workshops and knowledge sharing sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Provide technical guidance and review implementations
- **Product Managers:** Translate business requirements into technical solutions
- **Project Managers:** Advise on technical feasibility and effort estimates
- **QA:** Define testing strategies for architectural components
- **Risk & Compliance Lead:** Identify and address technical and security risks
- **Release Manager:** Ensure architectural integrity during deployments

---

## External Stakeholder

### Role Summary
External Stakeholders represent outside interests such as clients, regulatory authorities, partners, or vendors. They provide input on business objectives, validate deliverables, and ensure alignment with external requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate external requirements and expectations
- Provide feedback on deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Validate that solutions meet external business objectives
- Participate in milestone reviews and demos
- Escalate concerns or issues through appropriate channels
- Approve major deliverables and release decisions (when applicable)

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet external business needs
- Maintain alignment between project outputs and external requirements
- Provide timely feedback to keep projects on track
- Foster productive partnerships with delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and demo sessions
- Requirements gathering and validation sessions
- Status updates and progress reports from Project Managers
- Escalation communications for critical issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborate on requirements and priorities
- **Project Managers:** Receive status updates and provide feedback
- **Developers:** Validate technical solutions during demos
- **Release Manager:** Approve release timing and coordinate communications
- **Risk & Compliance Lead:** Review compliance requirements and risk posture
- **Change Manager:** Participate in change approval processes

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers oversee change requests and ensure all changes are properly documented, assessed for impact, and communicated effectively. They coordinate with teams to execute process adjustments smoothly and maintain change logs.

### Responsibilities
- Manage incoming change requests and prioritize based on impact
- Document changes and assess potential impacts on projects
- Coordinate change approval processes with stakeholders
- Communicate changes to affected teams and stakeholders
- Maintain change logs and audit trails
- Facilitate smooth transitions during process or system changes
- Monitor change success and identify improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Minimize disruption from changes to processes or systems
- Ensure all changes are properly evaluated and communicated
- Maintain clear change documentation and traceability
- Enable continuous improvement through managed change

### Typical Communication
- Change request review meetings with Project Managers
- Change impact assessments and approval requests
- Change notifications and implementation updates
- Post-change retrospectives and lessons learned

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Collaborate on change planning and impact assessment
- **Developers:** Coordinate technical changes and implementation
- **Product Managers:** Evaluate change priorities and business impact
- **Risk & Compliance Lead:** Assess risks associated with proposed changes
- **Release Manager:** Coordinate changes with release schedules
- **External Stakeholders:** Communicate changes that impact external parties
